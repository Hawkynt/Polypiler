Polypiler.Tooling/Grammar converts, from pegen (Tools/peg_generator/pegen of CPython 3.14.8), under
the PSF License Agreement for Python, https://docs.python.org/3/license.html#psf-license:
- GrammarReader.cs: the grammar format of metagrammar.gram (metas, rules, alternatives, items,
  actions and annotations);
- GrammarModel.cs: the grammar tree of grammar.py with its simple string forms;
- GrammarChecker.cs: the checks of parser_generator.py (rule and variable names, dangling
  references), NullableVisitor, InitialNamesVisitor, make_first_graph and compute_left_recursives,
  and strongly_connected_components and find_cycles_in_scc of sccutils.py (which pegen adapted from
  mypy, MIT);
- ParserEmitter.cs: the shape of the code c_generator.py emits (artificial rules for groups, loops
  and gathers, the alternatives of invalid rules, memoization, seed growing, cuts, lookaheads,
  forced tokens and default actions).

Summary of the changes: the Python is restated in C#; the emitter writes C# on
Polypiler.Core.Syntax instead of C on pegen.c, and the reader adds hooks, predicates, guards and
overlay directives (ADR 1000).

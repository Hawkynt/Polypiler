PB-Compiler recognizes the additive recurrences of the header phis of its SSA IR and simulates exact
trip counts of constant header tests. The conversion carries the model across to the structured loops
of the tree IR and adds affine values, symbolic and bounded counts and no-wrap flags; counts are
computed in closed form rather than simulated. PB-Compiler is licensed under the GNU Lesser General
Public License, version 3 or later. Terminology after Bachmann, Wang and Zima, "Chains of recurrences"
(ISSAC 1994).

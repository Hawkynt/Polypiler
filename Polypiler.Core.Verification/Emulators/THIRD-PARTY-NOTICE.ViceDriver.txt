ViceRunner.cs is converted to this codebase from the VICE test driver of PB-Compiler,
PowerBasic.Compiler.Tests/Exec/Vice.cs at commit 0d73acbea185a2597357d53f7ce9acb44758ba76
(https://github.com/Hawkynt/PB-Compiler), copyright Hawkynt. Taken from it: the headless start of
x64sc under Xvfb in warp mode with a dummy sound device, the autostart of the PRG injected into RAM,
the monitor tracepoint on the screen editor's character output at $E716 whose logged accumulator is
the program's screen output, and the BASIC READY. prompt that ends it. Added in the conversion: the
run through the sandboxed cross-language runner, the VIC-20 and C128 profiles, the tracepoints on
BASIC's READY routine with the memory saves and the debug-cartridge exit, and the keyboard input.

not distributed with this repository, nor are any ROM images.

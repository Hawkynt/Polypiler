Polypiler.Core.Frontends.Go converts, from the Go distribution, https://go.dev, under the following
licence:
- Syntax/GoScanner.cs: the scanner of src/go/scanner/scanner.go (next, scanComment, scanIdentifier,
  digits, scanNumber, invalidSep, scanEscape, scanRune, scanString, scanRawString, Scan);
- Syntax/GoParser.cs: the parser of src/go/parser/parser.go (every parse function, its look-ahead
  decisions and error messages, extractName, isTypeElem, packIndexExpr);
- Syntax/GoBuildConstraint.cs: minVersion of src/go/build/constraint/vers.go;
- Checking/GoConstant.cs: the representation and operations of src/go/constant/value.go (makeRat,
  makeFloatFromLiteral, smallFloat, ToInt, ToFloat, match, BinaryOp, UnaryOp, Shift, Compare,
  ExactString) and the 'p' format of src/math/big/ftoa.go;
- Checking/GoChecker*.cs: rules of src/go/types (representableConst, implicitTypeAndValue,
  updateExprType, matchTypes, comparison, shift, assignment, assignableTo, convertibleTo,
  isTerminating, hasBreak, blockBranches, the built-ins, compositeLit) restated for this checker;
- Checking/GoLibrary.cs: the constants of src/math/const.go and src/math/bits/bits.go verbatim, and
  the signatures of the exported functions of math and math/bits.

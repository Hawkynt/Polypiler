The helpers of Polypiler.Core.Backends.Rust/Helpers/BigInts.cs - the type PlyBigInt and the functions
ply_big_* - carry the algorithms of num-bigint 0.5.1 (commit 33c59ba44b7bdb09975b38a321b1b88c6a444005)
across into the safe Rust of Rust 1.31 that the generated crates are written in:

  src/bigint.rs                 the sign and magnitude of an integer, its order
  src/biguint/addition.rs       the addition of magnitudes with a carry (adc)
  src/biguint/subtraction.rs    the subtraction of magnitudes with a borrow (sbb)
  src/biguint/multiplication.rs the schoolbook multiplication of digits (mac_digit)
  src/biguint/division.rs       Knuth's algorithm D (div_rem_core), the division by one digit
                                (div_rem_digit), the normalization of the divisor
  src/biguint/shift.rs          the shifts of a magnitude by bits and by whole digits
  src/bigint/bits.rs            the two's complement of a negative integer digit by digit
                                (negate_carry), the bitwise operators on it
  src/bigint/shift.rs           the right shift of a negative integer rounding down

The conversion keeps 32-bit digits with 64-bit intermediates (num-bigint's u32 digit configuration),
replaces the inline assembly and intrinsics by the portable forms, and leaves out what the IR does not
use (Karatsuba and Toom-3 multiplication, Burnikel-Ziegler division, radix conversion beyond decimal
digits). Every generated crate that carries the type names num-bigint, its authors and its licence in
the header of src/lib.rs.

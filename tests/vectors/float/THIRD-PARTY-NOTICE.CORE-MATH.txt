The inputs of class "worst" in these vector files are taken from the worst-case files (*.wc) of
the CORE-MATH project (https://gitlab.inria.fr/core-math/core-math, commit
018fa97cbdacf59924b899a9dfa422d532cb2bb7): at most 400 lines of each, evenly spaced, from
src/binary64/<function>/<function>.wc and, for atan2, hypot and pow, src/binary32/<function>/<function>f.wc.
Those files collect hard-to-round cases from several sources, among them Vincent Lefèvre's
testlibm data (https://www.vinc17.net/research/testlibm/). The results are computed here with MPFR.

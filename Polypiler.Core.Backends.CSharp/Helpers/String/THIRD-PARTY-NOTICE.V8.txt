The helper ParseText of Polypiler.Core.Backends.CSharp/Helpers/String/CSharpNumberTextHelpers.cs reads the digits of parseInt in a radix other than 2, 4, 8, 10, 16 and 32 as V8 does,
which ECMA-262 leaves to the implementation for these radices: in runs whose multiplier stays within
32 bits, each run added to the number so far times its multiplier in double arithmetic, after the
leading zeros. Derived from NumberParseIntHelper::HandleGenericCase and
StringToIntHelper::DetectRadixInternal of src/numbers/conversions.cc of
https://github.com/v8/v8 at commit d83bc9e7a36426db1d059b3cfdc6cd910fafd87d.

The helper NumberText of Polypiler.Core.Backends.CSharp/Helpers/String/CSharpNumberTextHelpers.cs
finds the shortest digits of a double and of a float with Ryu, converted to C# 2 from
https://github.com/ulfjack/ryu at commit 4c0618b0e44f7ef027ebae05d2cc7812048f7c8f:

  ryu/d2s.c              d2d, d2d_small_int: the interval, the digits removed, the rounding
  ryu/d2s_small_table.h  DOUBLE_POW5_INV_SPLIT2, DOUBLE_POW5_SPLIT2, POW5_INV_OFFSETS, POW5_OFFSETS and
                         DOUBLE_POW5_TABLE (copied exactly), double_computePow5, double_computeInvPow5
  ryu/d2s_intrinsics.h   umul128, shiftright128, mulShift64, pow5Factor, multipleOfPowerOf2
  ryu/common.h           pow5bits, log10Pow2, log10Pow5

A float goes through the same computation with its own mantissa and exponent, which the 125-bit
powers of five hold exactly. Every generated file that carries the helper names Ryu, its author and
its licence in its header.

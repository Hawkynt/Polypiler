The tables of 2^(i/64) and 2^(i/4096), the fast polynomial of e^x and the accurate exponential,
which sinh.c, cosh.c and tanh.c each carry, are held once (Hyperbolic.Sinh.cs) where the copies
are identical; likewise the tables that sinhf.c and coshf.c share (Hyperbolic.cs).

The conversions round to nearest only: the code for the other rounding modes, the floating-point
exception flags and errno is left out. Every constant is copied exactly; the results agree bit
for bit with upstream on every binary32 input and on the worst cases of sinh.wc, cosh.wc and
tanh.wc.

Every table and constant is copied exactly; the hexadecimal floating-point literals of C are written
as the decimal literals that round-trip to the same double. Errno, floating-point exceptions and
rounding modes other than to nearest are left out. The table of reciprocals that logf.c, log2f.c
and log10f.c share is kept once, and so is the polynomial P that log.c and log10.c share. The
128-bit numbers of dint.h are held as four ulong and the carries of their additions are computed
without branches; log1p.c's refinement leaves out the branch for |x| < 2^-53, which its only
caller has answered already.

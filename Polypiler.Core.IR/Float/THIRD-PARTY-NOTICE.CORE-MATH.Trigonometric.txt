Parts of Polypiler.Core.IR/Float/Operations are converted to C# from CORE-MATH
(https://gitlab.inria.fr/core-math/core-math, commit 018fa97cbdacf59924b899a9dfa422d532cb2bb7):

  Trigonometric.Sine.cs    Sin    src/binary64/sin/sin.c (cr_sin)
                                  Copyright (c) 2026 Paul Zimmermann and Alexei Sibidanov
  Trigonometric.Sine.cs    SinF   src/binary32/sin/sinf.c (cr_sinf)
                                  Copyright (c) 2022-2025 Alexei Sibidanov.
  Trigonometric.Cosine.cs  Cos    src/binary64/cos/cos.c (cr_cos)
                                  Copyright (c) 2026 Paul Zimmermann
  Trigonometric.Cosine.cs  CosF   src/binary32/cos/cosf.c (cr_cosf)
                                  Copyright (c) 2022-2023 Alexei Sibidanov.
  Trigonometric.cs                the tables, argument reductions and fixed-point helpers that
                                  sin.c and cos.c, and sinf.c and cosf.c, carry identically

Every table and constant is copied exactly; hexadecimal floating-point literals are written as
the decimal literals that round-trip to the same double. Behaviour that only matters in the
directed rounding modes, errno and the floating-point exception flags are left out: the
conversions round to nearest. cos.c's table of four exceptions near 2^-22 is not carried, since
rounding to nearest never reaches it, nor the entry of sinf.c's table of exceptions for
0x1.2d97c8p+3, which cr_sinf never consults.

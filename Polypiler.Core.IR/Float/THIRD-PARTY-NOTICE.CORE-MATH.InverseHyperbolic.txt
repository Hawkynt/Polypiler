InverseHyperbolic.cs holds what these files have in common: the double-double helpers, the tables
of the logarithm that asinh.c, acosh.c and atanh.c each carry, the tables of the logarithm that
asinhf.c and acoshf.c each carry, and the databases of exceptional results of the three binary64
files, one after the other.

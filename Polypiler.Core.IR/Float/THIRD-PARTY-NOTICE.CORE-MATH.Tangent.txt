Parts of Polypiler.Core.IR/Float/Operations are converted to C# from the CORE-MATH project
(https://core-math.gitlabpages.inria.fr/, https://gitlab.inria.fr/core-math/core-math),
commit 018fa97cbdacf59924b899a9dfa422d532cb2bb7:

  Tangent.cs, Tangent.Accurate.cs, Tangent.Tables.cs  Tan   src/binary64/tan/tan.c
      Copyright (c) 2022-2025 Paul Zimmermann and Tom Hubrecht
  Tangent.cs                                          TanF  src/binary32/tan/tanf.c
      Copyright (c) 2022-2025 Alexei Sibidanov.

Every constant and table is copied exactly; hexadecimal floating-point literals are written as the
decimal literals of the same values. The 128-bit integers of the C code are carried as pairs of
64-bit words, its dint64_t numbers as arrays of four words, and its out-parameters as array
elements. Only rounding to nearest is kept, without errno and floating-point exceptions. Branches
that no finite argument reaches are left out: the zero upper word in set_dd() (the fraction of
x/(2pi) is at least 2^-62 for every double) and the overflowing and subnormal results in dint_tod()
(a tangent of a double lies between 2^-62 and 2^62 when it is not the argument itself).

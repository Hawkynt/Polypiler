                        as_ldexp, as_todenormal, t0, t1)

The constants and tables are copied exactly. Tables the sources repeat are kept once: the
double-double t0 and t1 of exp2.c, exp10.c and expm1.c are identical; exp.c differs from them
only in low parts, which it keeps as its own tables; the binary32 tables of 2^(i/2^6) and
2^(i/2^5) are the high parts of t0 and their even entries. The output parameters of the helpers
become one-element arrays, and the lists of exceptional cases find their entry by the same binary
search. expm1f.c's shortcut to -1 for x < -17.32 on its slower path is left out: rounding to
nearest, no float reaches it (checked on all 2^32 inputs).

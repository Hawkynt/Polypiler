The tables Asin and Acos have in common (asin.c and acos.c carry identical copies) are kept once,
in InverseTrigonometric.Asin.cs, and so are the two accurate polynomials asinf.c and acosf.c have
in common. Every constant is the exact value of the upstream one, written as a decimal literal.
